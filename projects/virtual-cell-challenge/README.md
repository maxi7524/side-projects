# Virtual Cell Challenge 2025

Material from team participation in Virtual Cell Challenge 2025. The competition is complete; see the [organizer’s wrap-up](https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2025-wrap-up). **Team result: 180th out of 337 ranked teams.** The result is recorded from the owner’s leaderboard screenshot and stated total; the full team name is truncated in that screenshot.

These retained notebooks document single-cell data exploration, GEARS training attempts and model-complexity analysis. They are not a complete archive of the team’s competition submission. The independent [team repository](https://github.com/mbozyk00/virtual-cells) remains separate.

Inputs, trained models and training logs remain outside Git. The original experiments are preserved in the [mithril-gears](experiments/mithril-gears) and [single-cell-training](experiments/single-cell-training) folders.
